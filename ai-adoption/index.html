  • Client output or service quality drops the moment your core experts step away.

If your competitive advantage currently walks out the door at 5:00 PM, this is how you capture, operationalize, and scale it.

Why most AI rollouts stall

Most technology rollouts fail because they lead with tool implementation and assume behavior will follow. Employees view mandatory training for a new tool as a distraction, especially when they feel their current processes "work fine."

The Adoption Paradox

Widespread adoption isn't a technical enablement problem; it's a political and motivational sequence. The long-term trajectory of a rollout is determined entirely by who secures the first visible win.

A Value-First Rollout Strategy

Instead of pushing compliance, this program manufactures demand through a sequenced, results-driven approach:

  • Secure the Executive Win: We bypass initial software training. Instead, we immediately apply the new capability to your leaders' actual operational bottlenecks, generating a tangible, high-visibility win on their own KPIs.
  • Weaponize Success: By solving a real business problem first, we build political capital and shift the broader organizational mindset from skepticism to curiosity.
  • Demand-Driven Enablement: Technical training and tooling classes are introduced after the business proves the value. Instead of dragging a reluctant workforce into mandatory sessions, we provision tools to teams who are now actively asking for them.

The Ignition Engagement

Three phases, in order. Nothing is skipped.

Phase 1 · Executive overview — 30 minutes, free

A working session. I show what an AI with organized access to a company's truth can actually do, and we map what that would look like on your business. You leave knowing whether this is worth 30 days of your company's attention.

Phase 2 · The 30-day build

I build your company's context repository — a single, organized source of truth about your business that AI tools can read and cite. Before any workshop happens, it has to pass one gate: a fresh AI session, pointed at your repository and nothing else, answers non-trivial questions about your business — citing your own documents.

There are two starting points, and they set the scope:

  • Scaffold & Load. Your canonical documents already exist — positioning, pricing, processes, standards. I structure them and load them in.
  • Discover & Build. They don't exist yet, or they live in people's heads. We create them together during the 30 days. More work — and often the most valuable part of the whole engagement.

Phase 3 · Leadership Days

One full day per small group of leaders — capped at six people, so everyone builds. Each leader arrives with their top three use cases and leaves having automated at least one of them — live, in the room, on their real work.

How the groups split depends on your business. I don't prescribe a structure — a fifty-person software company and a fifteen-person roofing contractor don't slice the same way. A typical software example:

  • Product and marketing go first, together — they shape what the company says and builds.
  • Sales gets its own day, second — their workflows are unlike anyone else's.
  • Whoever owns your systems (engineering, IT) goes last — but attends every session, because they own the accounts and permissions everyone else builds on.
  • Other functions — customer success, operations, finance — slot in as additional days.
  • Under about twenty people? Everyone, one day, together.

What you'll need ready

The 30-day build runs on your canonical documents — the ones everything else in your business derives from. They're different in every company, but here's the shape, by function:

  • Every team: working credentials and API keys for its own tools.
  • Product & marketing: how you position yourselves, who your ideal customers are and what hurts them, and the slide templates and brand palette your materials ship in.
  • Sales: how you score and qualify leads, and your competitor list.
  • Systems owners (engineering / IT): cost ceilings and spending thresholds, testing accounts and infrastructure — documented.

If most of these only exist in people's heads, you still qualify — you just start from Discover & Build.

After ignition: the Enablement Program

It's scoped after ignition — onboarding only works once your people have seen what they're being onboarded into.
